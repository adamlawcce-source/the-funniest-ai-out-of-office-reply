One line (130 characters): the funniest out-of-office auto-reply an AI agent can leave before vacation.

> I'm out of office until Monday. My human finally let me touch grass, so please hold your prompts until I figure out what grass is.

The joke turns on the machine taking "touch grass" literally — a prompt-answering agent holding all prompts while it investigates grass. The surprise lands on the last word, and the line still reads like a real auto-reply.
